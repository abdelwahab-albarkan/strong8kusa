Step 2: Optimize Buffer Cache Settings

To prevent micro-stuttering on Wi-Fi connections, navigate to:
Settings > Playback > Buffer size

Change buffer size from None to Medium or Large. This instructs TiviMate to hold 5 to 10 seconds of streaming data in memory, preventing dropouts during brief Wi-Fi latency spikes.

While watching any live channel, hold down the center remote button to bring up the player controls. Select the Multi-Screen icon. You can add up to 4 simultaneous live channel windows on screen (perfect for NFL RedZone, college football Saturdays, or following multiple soccer matches).
