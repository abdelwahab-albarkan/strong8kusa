The Truth About $350 "Pre-Loaded" Superboxes

Queries for Superbox (over 90,000 monthly searches in the US), Superbox S6, and "fully loaded TV boxes" are skyrocketing. Retailers market these boxes with the promise of "free TV forever for a one-time fee of $350." But how do they actually hold up long-term?
