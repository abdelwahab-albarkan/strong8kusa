How to Setup IPTV on Amazon Firestick in 5 Minutes

The Amazon Fire TV Stick is America's most popular streaming player. This complete guide walks you through installing Downloader, setting up TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro, and streaming 25,000+ live 4K channels without buffering.

1

Install the Downloader Application

Because dedicated IPTV media players are not hosted on Amazon's native app store, you must first install the verified Downloader utility:

  1. From your Firestick home screen, navigate to the Find (Magnifying Glass) icon.
  2. Type Downloader using the on-screen keyboard.
  3. Select the orange Downloader icon published by AFTVnews.com.
  4. Click Get or Download and wait for installation to finish.
2

Enable Developer Options on Fire OS

On recent Amazon Fire OS updates, Developer Options are hidden by default. Follow these steps to reveal them:

  1. Go to Firestick Settings (Gear Icon) > My Fire TV > About.
  2. Highlight the name of your device (e.g. Fire TV Stick 4K) and click the center remote button 7 times rapidly until a message says "No need, you are already a developer."
  3. Press Back once to return to My Fire TV — you will now see Developer Options.
  4. Click Install Unknown Apps, find Downloader, and toggle it to ON.
3

Download Your IPTV Player (TiviMate or Smarters)

Launch the Downloader app and enter one of these direct numeric shortcodes in the URL bar:

TiviMate IPTV Player (Recommended for Firestick):

Downloader Code: 272483

IPTV Smarters Pro:

Downloader Code: 78522

Downloader will automatically download the APK file. When prompted, click Install, then click Open.

4

Connect Your Strong 8K USA Account

Once the app opens, connect your credentials:

  1. Select Add Playlist > Xtream Codes API (this is much faster and more reliable than typing long M3U URLs).
  2. Enter any playlist name (e.g. Strong 8K USA).
  3. Enter the Server URL provided in your welcome email or free trial: http://line.strong8kusa.com:80.
  4. Enter your Username and Password.
  5. Click Next or Login. The player will download your 25,000+ live channel guide in under 30 seconds.
